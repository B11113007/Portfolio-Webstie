BIOGRAPHY & CREDENTIALS

Profile

Yuan-Hao Lin is an architecture researcher and designer focusing on resilient urban infrastructures, material re-use, and spatial phenomenology in subtropical climates.

Having completed M.Arch studies with distinction, his work investigates the intersection between precise structural engineering and soft environmental boundaries. Prior to graduate research, he worked with award-winning practices on public cultural centers and experimental housing prototypes.
